Refunds:
Refunds are processed within 7 business days after approval.

Shipping:
Standard delivery usually takes 3-5 business days.
Tracking details are sent by email after the order ships.

Invoices:
Invoices are sent by email after purchase.
Customers can also request another invoice copy from support.

Returns:
Returns must be made within 14 days.
Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Support:
Customer support is available Monday to Friday during business hours.
